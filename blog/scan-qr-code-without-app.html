How to Scan a QR Code Without Downloading Any App

By Ali · QR Studio Blog

I get asked this a lot by family members who see a QR code on a poster or a restaurant table and immediately assume they need to go download some app first. You almost never do. Most phones made in the last several years can scan QR codes using nothing but the regular camera app that's already installed.

On an iPhone

  1. Open the regular Camera app — the same one you use for photos.
  2. Point it at the QR code, no need to tap anything or take a photo.
  3. A yellow notification banner pops up at the top with a link — tap it.

On most Android phones

  1. Open the Camera app.
  2. Point it at the code and hold still for a second.
  3. A small popup appears on screen — tap it to open the link.

If nothing happens on Android, swipe down from the top of the screen — many phones (Samsung, Pixel, and others) have a "Scan QR code" quick-settings button you can turn on there.

If your camera really doesn't support it

A small number of older or budget phones don't have this built in. In that case, the fastest fix isn't installing a random scanner app from the app store (many of those are packed with ads or ask for unnecessary permissions) — it's using a scanner that runs straight in your browser, like QR Studio. Open it once, tap "Scan," allow camera access, and you're done. No install, no account.

Why I stopped recommending scanner apps

A few years ago I'd tell people to just download any QR scanner app. Then a relative installed one that turned out to be full of ads and asked for permissions it had no business needing — contacts, location, the works, just to read a square image. Since then I only recommend either the built-in phone camera, or a browser-based scanner that doesn't need installing or storing anything on your phone.
