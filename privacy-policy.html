Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 3, 2026

QR Studio ("the app") is a QR code scanner and generator. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is used.

Camera

The app uses your device camera only to detect a QR code on screen, entirely on-device. Camera frames are never recorded, stored, or sent anywhere.

Data stored on your device

Scan and creation history, your theme preference, and your settings are stored locally on your device using standard app storage. This data is never transmitted to us or to any third party, and you can clear it at any time from Settings → Clear History.

Advertising

The free version of this app shows ads served by Google AdMob. Ad networks may collect a device advertising identifier and general usage data to show and measure ads, and may set their own cookies or identifiers. You can review Google's practices at policies.google.com/technologies/ads. Purchasing "Remove Ads" turns off ad requests entirely.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your payment details.

Children

This app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Terms of Use

QR Studio is provided "as is," free to use, with an optional paid upgrade for unlimited tracked QR codes and an ad-free experience. You are responsible for the content you encode into or scan with QR codes — do not use the app to create or distribute illegal, harmful, or misleading content. We may update the app and these terms from time to time; continued use after an update means you accept the changes.
